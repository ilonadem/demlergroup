Duilio De Santis receives the “Paolo Mazzoldi” Award from the Italian Physical Society

Award citation: "For his innovative contributions demonstrating how far-from-equilibrium dynamics, fluctuations, and dissipation can foster the emergence of new collective states and phenomena of great physical relevance." The award ceremony will take place on September 7 during the opening ceremony of the 112th National Congress of the Italian Physical Society, hosted by the University of Florence. Congratulations, Duilio!

February 2026

Registration is open for the ECO-NOISE 2026 conference in Erice, Italy

Our group organises the conference "Emergence of Coherence in Nonequilibrium and Structured Environments" (ECO-NOISE 2026) in Erice, Italy, July 23-28, 2026. The conference will focus on how external coherent driving or structured noise through cavities can be leveraged to enhance coherence in many-body systems.
We invite everybody interested to apply!
