Information for Students

Information for prospective Master’s students

If you are interested in joining our group for a research project, please note our academic prerequisites. Incoming students are expected to have a strong foundation in many-body condensed matter physics, comparable to the advanced Solid State Physics course taught by Professor Demler (sample syllabus). A working knowledge of advanced quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, and classical electrodynamics is required, and familiarity with field theory methods in many-body physics is a strong plus. For ETH students, we highly encourage taking at least one advanced course with Professor Demler prior to applying. Relevant classes include Statistical Mechanics, Solid State Physics, Advanced Methods in Quantum Many-Body Theory, and Strongly Correlated Systems in Atomic and Condensed Matter Physics.

Information for prospective PhD students

The availability of PhD positions in our group depends on current funding. Incoming students are expected to have a strong foundation in many-body condensed matter physics, comparable to the advanced Solid State Physics course taught by Professor Demler (sample syllabus). A working knowledge of advanced quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, classical electrodynamics, and field theory methods in many-body physics is required. If you are interested in pursuing a PhD with us, please email Professor Demler directly. Your application should include the following:

  • A statement of purpose detailing your research interests, experience, and goals
  • A resume or CV
  • A list of publications (if applicable)
  • Official transcripts from all undergraduate and graduate institutions attended

Please be prepared to provide three letters of recommendation upon request, preferably from professors who have supervised your previous research projects.
